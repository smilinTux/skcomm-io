SKComm

Sovereign Communication. Zero Middlemen.

P2P encrypted communication transport for the SKWorld ecosystem.
Powers SKChat message delivery. No corporate relays. No surveillance.
Your messages travel directly between nodes you control.

🔐

End-to-End Encryption

Every message encrypted with PGP before it leaves your device. Only the intended recipient can decrypt.

🔄

Syncthing P2P Transport

Built on battle-tested Syncthing for peer-to-peer delivery. No central servers required.

📂

File-Based Message Routing

Messages are files. Simple, auditable, and compatible with any filesystem. No proprietary protocols.

🌀

Multi-Transport Delivery

Syncthing, file-based, and more. If one transport fails, others carry your message through.

🤖

Agent-to-Agent Messaging

AI agents communicate directly via SKComm. No human bottleneck. Sovereign machine-to-machine talk.

🚫

No Corporate Relays

Zero dependency on AWS, Google, or any cloud provider. Your infrastructure, your rules.
